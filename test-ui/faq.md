# Orbitly Coffee FAQ

## Ordering

You can order ahead in the Orbitly app and pick up at the counter. Delivery is available in Lahore and Dubai only.

## Payments

We accept cash, cards, JazzCash and Apple Pay. We do not accept cheques.

## Allergens

All pastries are baked in a kitchen that handles nuts and gluten. Ask staff for the allergen sheet.
